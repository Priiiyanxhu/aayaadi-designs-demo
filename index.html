From first call to handover

Sample copy for this concept. The studio's own process may differ.

Call the studio
  1. 1

    Talk and visit

    It starts with a call about the rooms you have in mind, then a visit to the home to see the light, the sizes and how the space is used day to day.

    Walk-through
    A visit to see the rooms, take measurements and note what stays.
    Daily routines
    How you cook, host, work and store things, room by room.
    First scope
    Which rooms are in, and what each one needs to do.

    You leave withA clear scope for the rooms in question

  2. 2

    Plan and materials

    Layouts and joinery drawings take shape alongside real samples, so stone, timber, fabric and brass are judged in your own light, not on a screen.

    Layout and joinery
    Plans and drawings for wardrobes, panelling and storage.
    Material samples
    Stone, timber profiles, laminates, fabrics and handles to hold and compare.
    Lighting plan
    Where the coves, backlit walls and dimmable circuits go.

    You leave withDrawings and a material palette to approve

  3. 3

    Build and handover

    Carpentry, stone, paint and lighting are made and fitted on site. Before handover, the rooms are walked together and anything unfinished is noted and fixed.

    Site coordination
    Regular visits while joinery, stone and electrical work are fitted.
    Snag walk-through
    A close look at hinges, edges, finishes and alignments.
    Handover
    Final styling and a guided walk through the finished home.

    You leave withFinished rooms, walked through together

What kinds of spaces does the studio design?

Homes in and around Gurugram, from single rooms such as a bedroom or study to full apartments.

How do I start a project?

Call the studio or message them on Instagram. A first conversation covers the rooms, your timeline and how you use the space.

Do you work with my own furniture and pieces?

Yes. Existing furniture and favourite pieces can be planned into the new layout.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the home and the scope of carpentry. A timeline is agreed after the site visit.

Can I see the studio's work in person?

The studio is at DLF Phase 2, Gurugram. Call ahead to arrange a visit.
